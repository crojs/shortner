About ShortURL Pro

ShortURL Pro is a free, powerful, and privacy-focused URL shortener designed to make your long links manageable, trackable, and shareable. We support the clck.ru domain and provide local browser-based fallback for custom short codes.

Our Mission

We believe that everyone should have access to simple, fast, and secure link shortening tools without compromising privacy. Our tool runs entirely in your browser, ensuring your data never leaves your device.

Core Features

  • Instant Shortening – Shorten any URL with a single click.
  • AI-Powered Analysis – Detect suspicious patterns and classify links.
  • QR Code Generation – Generate and download QR codes for any short link.
  • Privacy First – All data stored locally, no account required.
  • Full History – Keep track of all your shortened links.

Why Choose Us?

Unlike other URL shorteners, ShortURL Pro does not require registration, respects your privacy, and provides enterprise-level features for free.
