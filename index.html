About ShortURL Pro

ShortURL Pro is a free, premium URL shortener supporting multiple short domains — is.gd, v.gd, gg.gg, tny.im, clck.ru, and da.gd. Pick your preferred service or let our AI auto-select the best option. Fully responsive, AI-powered, and SEO-optimized.

Supported Short URL Services

is.gd — Very short domain, reliable.
v.gd — Similar to is.gd, alternative.
gg.gg — Short and easy to remember.
tny.im — Ultra-short domain.
clck.ru — Short Russian-based service.
da.gd — Short and fast.

Privacy Policy

ShortURL Pro respects your privacy. All URL shortening operations are performed locally in your browser. We do not store or transmit your data to external servers. Your link history is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage.

How It Works

Select your preferred short domain from the dropdown, paste your URL, and click Shorten. If you select "Auto", the tool tries all services in order until one succeeds. For custom codes, local browser links are used to maintain full control.
