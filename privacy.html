Privacy Policy

Last updated:

At ShortURL Pro, accessible from https://olllo.netlify.app, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by ShortURL Pro and how we use it.

Information We Collect

We do not require any personal information to use our URL shortening service. However, we may store non-personal data locally in your browser (using localStorage) to provide features like link history and theme preference. This data never leaves your device.

Third-Party Services

Our website may use third-party services such as Google AdSense, which may use cookies and web beacons to display ads based on your prior visits. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

Cookies

We do not directly use cookies, but third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your browsing history. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings.

Data Security

All URL shortening operations are performed locally in your browser. We do not transmit or store your long URLs on any external server. Your history is kept in your local browser storage and can be cleared at any time using the "Clear All History" button.

Children's Information

We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child provided information on our website, please contact us immediately and we will remove it.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@olllo.netlify.app.
